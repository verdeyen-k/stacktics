    How to play

    • Take turns dropping a block onto the tower. Drag anywhere to slide it, use ⟲ ⟳ to tilt it, then hit Drop.
    • Every block that falls off the platform gives ★1 to everyone whose turn it isn't.
    • Spend ★ on power-ups for your block: 🤏 Tiny, 🐘 Huge, ↔️ Long (doubles the length, buy it again to keep stretching), 🍯 Glue (sticks to whatever it touches), 🧽 Slippery (a soapy sponge that makes what it touches slide), 🏀 Bouncy (hops once when it lands, then flings off anything that lands on it), 🪱 Flexible (a rubbery block that bends and stretches), 🧲 Magnet (pulls nearby blocks in), ⏩ Rush (the next player's turn starts before the tower settles — anything that falls is on them).
    • First to ★10 wins. Spending is a gamble!
    • Want to see how the pieces behave? 🔬 Science mode has no timers or scores, every power-up is free, and 🧹 clears the platform.
